The agent

  1. Step A — gather: Composio Instant search/fetch first, free fallback second. Pages are cached; every call is counted.
  2. Step B — pass 1: three small extraction calls per app (auth/API, access, MCP). Every claim needs a URL and a snippet.
  3. Step C — pass 2: one batched claim check per app, a code-only snippet-in-page check, and consistency rules.
